# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and precise. Assume
good intent while discussing technical issues, and welcome differing views.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
unwelcome personal contact are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation for behavior that violates this standard. Report concerns
privately to [support@isogate.tech](mailto:support@isogate.tech) with links or
other context where available.
